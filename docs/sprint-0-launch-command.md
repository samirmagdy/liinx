# RALOA — Sprint 0 Launch Command

## Company objective
Get **25 real creators/service businesses to publish and actively use a RALOA page within 30 days**.

This sprint is not measured by features shipped, signups, traffic, or visual polish. It is measured by **activated publishers**.

## ICP for this sprint
KSA/UAE/Egypt freelancers and service professionals:
- photographers
- coaches/consultants
- fitness trainers
- beauty professionals
- designers/freelancers
- small agencies

## Product promise
**Turn your social profile into a professional bilingual mini-site that generates inquiries, bookings and sales — in minutes.**

## North-star activation event
`first_publish_completed`

## Core funnel
Landing → Signup → Goal selection → Template → Content → Publish → Share → Public CTA

## Day-1 operating rules
1. No major feature expansion.
2. Every team decision must improve activation, retention, conversion, or learning.
3. Real user evidence beats internal opinion.
4. No fake testimonials, metrics, logos, scarcity, or unsupported claims.
5. Arabic/English and RTL are product features, not post-launch localization.
6. Paid acquisition remains off until one segment/channel shows repeatable activation.

## Team missions
- Founder/CEO: personally recruit and onboard real users.
- Product/UX: reduce time-to-publish below 5 minutes.
- Engineering: instrument the activation funnel and remove technical blockers.
- Design: design the guided onboarding and first-publish experience.
- Growth: build the first 100-prospect pipeline and test niches manually.
- Localization: make Arabic/English/RTL production quality.
- QA: enforce launch gates around publishing, billing, analytics and RTL.

## Week-1 success criteria
- 100 qualified prospects identified.
- 20 customer conversations.
- 10 real published RALOA pages.
- Median test time-to-publish < 5 minutes.
- Complete event coverage for the activation funnel.
- Zero P0 defects in the core publish journey.
- Top 5 objections and top 5 desired outcomes documented.

## Decision checkpoint
At the end of week 1:
- Double down on the segment with the strongest publish/share behavior.
- Fix the largest activation bottleneck.
- Reject roadmap items not supported by repeated customer evidence.
